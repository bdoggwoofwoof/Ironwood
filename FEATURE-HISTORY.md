# Ironwood feature history

Until Nov 10, 2026 the app was built by features.py: 45 numbered find-and-replace steps applied to source.html (the Oct 9 Claude artifact version). On Nov 10 the result was split into normal source files (index.src.html, styles.css, boot.js, core.js and the feature modules) and assembled by assemble.py, producing a byte-for-byte identical app.html. These are the notes each step carried, kept for history. Both old files are in git history before commit 'Fold the patch steps into modules'.

- 1. Name
- 2. Photo backup helpers, placed right after phDelete
- 3. Profile: photo backup button (two steps, so the save happens right on the tap)
- 4. Restore sheet: either backup type, big files straight from the file picker
- 5. First launch: offer Restore while there's nothing logged yet (hidden after a workout or a tap on "Not now")
- 6. Routine library: more ready-made programs, and library routines can be edited or removed. An edited library routine is saved under the same id (with base: id), so it keeps its workout history and "next up" order. Removed ones are listed in meta.hidden and can be brought back.
- 8. Newt's 4-Day Split as he edited it in the app (Oct 10): rep ranges, new supersets, no pull-ups on legs day, plain day names.
- 7. Exercise notes on routines (shown in the routine and during the workout), workout-type tags in the editor.
- 9. Quick-log other activities (running, swimming, yoga, BJJ…). Saved as a session with kind 'activity' and no exercises, so streaks, the week strip, workouts-per-week and backups all count it with no further changes.
- 10. Train / History (Log moves under Train), choosing a program or Self-log, dismissable tips, Help (tour + feedback) on every page, the save status as a labeled line in History, and color themes (two color-blind friendly).
- 11. Sheet handles work: drag the bar down to close the sheet, up to make it full height (tap toggles the size).
- 12. Meal prep: a Meals tab with recipe ideas and ready-made preps, scaling by people / servings / weeks, a shopping list added up and sorted by aisle, and your own recipes (meals.js). Saved in meta.meals, so backups carry it.
- 13. Running and conditioning (Oct 21, 2026; run.js): exercises logged by time, distance and splits (planks, hangs, runs, carries); Train → Run with paces, a profile and 8-week targets from the 100 m, mile and 3 mile, the 8-week plan for 2 or 3 runs a week next to the lifting days, session targets on the workout screen, on/off target checks, week 4 and week 8 tests that update times; cross-training swaps; six conditioning programs with timers; running PRs and stats. Saved in meta.run and on each set (t, d, sp), so backups carry it. Nothing leaves the phone.
- 14. Oct 21 review improvements (improve.js; review in the Launch Plan's Review findings tab): progression (jump per exercise, smallest jump, smaller jump after a miss, ~10% drop after three misses, target reps, last time's sets on each exercise), older holds logged as reps, IndexedDB copy of the data, weekly goal from the program, repeat / save a past workout, body measurements, share a workout picture; running plan adapts (missed runs, easy week, push back), goal race, quick-logged runs count (run.js).
- 15. Joint care programs (care.js): seven two-day strength and mobility programs for common aches (shoulders, ankles and shins, lower back, knees, hips, elbows and wrists, neck and upper back), with a pain rule, signs to see a doctor or physical therapist, and no therapy or treatment claims. A finder on Routines; a pointer from Run → Programs.
- 16. Programs together (together.js): a main program plus others alongside (joint care, conditioning, any plan), and the running plan, all in Train; an add-on's next day can be added to the main workout or done on its own.
- 19. Fight training (fight.js, Oct 25): Train → Fight with a coach that calls combos out loud for boxing, kickboxing, Muay Thai and MMA (or solo BJJ movements) over timed rounds, a combo library with favorites, drills and session ideas, and six fight programs in Routines. Coach sessions save to History as activities with rounds, or fill a program workout's rounds. Settings in meta.fight. Nothing leaves the phone.
- 17. Tour (Oct 21): covers programs together, weights that adjust, running plan options, joint care, History actions, measurements and the meal log. Replaces the whole step list.
- 18. Reps don't carry forward (Oct 26, Newt's request). Finishing a set used to copy its reps into every set after it; now later sets keep the reps from last time (or the routine's target), and only the weight carries forward.
- 20. Your week (schedule.js, Oct 27): a weekly schedule for everything you follow (main program, programs alongside, running plan), dots on the week strip (two on a doubled-up day), Also on your plan says when each is next, and a calendar file (.ics) for Apple, Google, Outlook or Proton Calendar.
- 21. Layout fixes (layout.js, Oct 27): challenge templates you can find (Train card while you have none, Routines, a sheet with Iron 75, Iron 75 Lite, 30-Day Streak, 6-Week Protein & Sleep, or your own), and one header style: every main page starts with the same big left title row (Stats and Profile used a small centered title, and the two-option switch on Routines was bigger than the four-option ones).
- 22. Barcode scan and online food lookup (foodscan.js, Oct 27; Newt chose opt-in online lookups): Log food → Scan reads a barcode with the camera (ZXing WebAssembly on iPhone, vendored in vendor/ and loaded on first use) or a typed number, matches your own foods first (works offline), and with lookups allowed asks Open Food Facts for the barcode or a name search. Only the barcode or search words are sent; a logged product is saved to your foods. Also: every page is at least one screen tall, so the tab bar sits at the very bottom on short pages (on the iPhone home-screen app it rose about 10 pt on the Meals log, the only page shorter than the screen).
- 23. Last time's numbers and rep records (prefill.js, Oct 27, Newt's requests): prefilled weights and reps come from the last time in any workout (default) or only in the same workout / plan day (Profile → Progression, or per plan on its page). Each set shows its numbers from last time beside it, since reps aimed at the range can make every set read the same. Exercise stats list the best reps at each weight (heaviest first; lb and kg converted).
- 24. Lift goals, month in review, prep rotation (growth.js + meals.js, Oct 29; from Newt's reels notes): a goal per lift (est. 1RM or heaviest by a date) shown on Train with progress, the weekly gain needed and whether the recent pace gets there; a month-in-review recap (Train card the first week of a month, Stats → Month in review) with a note and a share picture; saved preps and a 2–4 prep weekly rotation that loads each Monday.
- 25. Tour (Oct 29): new stops for during a workout, challenges, lift goals and month in review, meal prep rotation, the food log with barcode scan and lookup, and backups. A step can switch the Meals page (extra.meals).
- 26. Meal plan from your targets (mealplan.js, Nov 2, Newt's request): height, weight, age, activity and goal (optional BMI) set daily calories and macros (estimates with a floor), then a week of meals with portions that can fill the prep and shopping list; today's plan on the food log with one-tap logging. Amounts everywhere in Meals can be Easy (3 chicken breasts, half a can, about a fist), as written, or by weight; spices and sauces stay exact.
- 27. Weight is total or each side (Nov 2, Newt's request): a dot button by the weight heading of each exercise. One big dot = the total weight; two small dots = the weight on each side (cable flys, dumbbells). Kept per exercise in meta.exSide and shown on the Last line. A label only: PRs and charts compare the numbers as entered.
- 28. Machines (machines.js, Nov 2, Newt's request): name the machines you use for an exercise (⋯ → Machines); chips on the exercise switch machine mid-lift. Each set keeps its machine; weights, Last numbers, weight carry-over and PRs compare only sets on the same machine.
- 29. Food log, simpler (logfind.js, Nov 2, Newt's request): Log food opens on one search box for everything (recipes, your foods, built-in foods, "2 eggs"), with Scan and Enter numbers beside it; before you type, Recent (tap to log the same amount again) and Saved foods by category (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks, Drinks or your own). A logged entry can be saved or logged again. With a meal plan, the log shows today's plan instead of a second prep list.
- 30. Turn plans off or restart them, and a check-in when a week slips (planctl.js, Nov 2, Newt's request): every plan you follow can be stopped or restarted (plan page, Run tab); when 2+ planned sessions are missed in a week, or a plan was never started, Train asks: I did them / Restart / Postpone / Edit / Turn off.
- 31. Running plan setup that fits your lifting (runfit.js, Nov 2, Newt's request): times are optional (pick a level and start from estimates); the plan reads the main program's days and asks how runs fit (mixed / days off / lifting days / running first); programs added alongside ask which days too.
- 32. Food preferences, recipe sources, recipes from a post, and recipes from Newt's reels (foodprefs.js + meals.js, Nov 2): allergies and diet style block recipes from plans and flag them; dislikes stay out of plans; each recipe can link its source (shown at the bottom); "From a post" fills a recipe from a pasted caption.
- 33. Iron 75 / Lite from a questionnaire (iron75.js, Nov 2): priorities and habits build the checklist; protein and calories tick from the food log; a sick or hurt day can be a light day instead of breaking the run.
- 34. Goal plans (goals.js, Nov 2): an interview (goal, sport, keep my program or a new one, days, minutes, gear, cardio, joints, winter bulk → spring cut) builds your own copy of one of six built-in plans or adjusts around your program; it flows into the meal plan setup; season switches are asked first.
- 35. Eating habits and first-launch setup (Nov 2): meals a day, how often you cook, keep it cheap, quick recipes shape the meal plan; when the tour ends on a fresh install, Ironwood offers Build my plan once.
- 36. Edit a food you entered by hand (Nov 3, Newt's request): a logged entry made with Enter numbers opens with an Edit button; the name and every number can be changed. If the food is also saved in a category, the saved copy changes with it, so logging it again uses the new numbers. Undo puts the old entry back.
- 37. Organization plan (Nov 3, Newt's picks in the Ironwood Organization Plan doc). Structure, order and words only: no new styles. A1 one word, "plan", for anything you follow (main plan + plans alongside). A2 the Programs alongside sheet becomes "Your plan": main plan, alongside, running plan and challenges in one list. A3 the Plans library is grouped by kind (yours, lifting, goal, running and conditioning, fight, joint care, challenges). B1/B2 Train is Train, Run, Fight; History moves to the Stats tab, renamed Progress, as its first section. B3 the Routines tab is named Plans. B4 one door for logging an activity (week strip, History). C1 Up next before the month review, goals and restore prompt. C2 your routines and the library first on Plans. C3 Backup first and Help last on Profile. C4 Challenges live in the Plans library, and on Train only while one runs. D1 the empty Run screen and the meal-plan card point to Build my plan too. D3 each tab opens with one line saying what it is for. Doc notes: See your month and Restore a backup use the gray button; Stats shows three numbers with More numbers for the rest; colors are Classic, Forest (a new woodsy gym theme, surfaces too) and Color-blind friendly (two kinds), plus Custom.
- 38. Review my eating (eatreview.js, Nov 3, Newt's request): Meals → Log → Review my eating, and Progress → Overall → Nutrition. Averages against goals, where calories, sodium and sugar come from, time of day, training vs rest days, food groups behind the nutrients the app can't count, and suggestions with recipes to add to your prep.
- 39. Workout clock in the header (Nov 3, Newt's request): once the big elapsed clock scrolls under the header, the same time shows at the header's top left, so it's visible anywhere in the workout. Same font and color as the big clock.
- 40. Tighter top and bottom, bigger section switch (Nov 3, Newt's request from a phone screenshot). Less empty space under the status bar (the page already sits below it, so the spacer no longer adds the status bar a second time) and below the tab bar on every page; Train/Run/Fight and the other top switches 30px on every page (Meals has four and still fits; 27px after Newt found 30 a bit big); Progress and Profile titles match. Tab bar icons and labels keep their size.
- 41. Read a nutrition label (labelread.js, Nov 3, Newt's request: scanning wasn't finding his foods). New food / Edit food gets a "Read the label" card: a photo of the Nutrition Facts panel is read on the phone (Tesseract.js in vendor/ocr, loaded on first use) or label text pasted from iPhone Live Text, and the numbers fill the fields to check before saving. Scan gets "Read a nutrition label"; a barcode that wasn't found keeps its number, so the next scan finds the saved food offline.
- 42. Sheets stay above the iPhone keyboard (Nov 3, Newt's report: typing in Log food's search, the sheet sat behind the keyboard). The keyboard covers the screen without resizing the page, so the sheet's backdrop now follows the visible area (visualViewport) while the keyboard is up, and the field being typed in scrolls into view.
- 43. Change how much you ate (Nov 4, Newt's request: eating two of something meant logging it twice). Every logged food, today or on any earlier day, gets an Amount stepper (× what was logged, in halves up to 20); recipes keep their Servings stepper. Logging from Recent or Saved shows "Undo" and "Change amount" in the message. Toasts can carry a second button (action2 / fn2), same look as the first.
- 44. Last photo in red, no outline on top (Nov 9, Newt's request). In a check-in, the last photo of the same pose shows tinted red so it's clear which one is you now and which is last time; while it shows, the standard pose outline and its level lines are hidden. Slide "Last photo" to 0% to get the outline back.
- 45. Level for check-in photos (tilt.js, Nov 9, Newt's request). With the live camera, "Show level" reads the phone's motion sensor (iPhone asks once): an iPhone-Camera-style level (thin line whose centre turns with the phone, a fixed + with a floating + for the remaining angle, yellow when lined up) and plain directions, green when the phone matches the last photo's angle, or level when there's no earlier angle. Each live photo saves its tilt (rec.tilt) for next time.
- 46. Audit P0 fixes (Nov 10, Newt's Yes in the Ironwood App Audit). (1) Text on accent buttons is picked by contrast in every palette (train.js inkFor/palVars), so the color-blind themes' dark Start/Log food/Finish buttons pass 4.5:1. (2) Rest bar buttons sit on a darker (or, with dark text, lighter) shade of the accent, and the Rest label is full strength. (3) Each set number is a button that opens Log/Clear, Copy set and Delete set (with Undo), so a set in the middle no longer needs a swipe. (4) Schedule strip cells lose the table roles that hid their state.
