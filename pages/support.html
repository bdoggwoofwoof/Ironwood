Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue with what happened and what phone you use.

Install Ironwood on your iPhone

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Ironwood from its new icon. After the first open it works with no signal.

Keep your data safe

  • Your workouts and photos live only on your phone.
  • Go to Profile → Backup → Save backup file every week or two, and save it to Files or iCloud Drive.
  • Deleting the home-screen icon deletes all of Ironwood's data. Save a backup first.

Move to a new phone

  1. On the old phone: Profile → Save backup file.
  2. On the new phone: install Ironwood, then Profile → Restore and pick the file.

Restore adds the backup's workouts and routines to what's already there. It never overwrites anything.

Common questions

The app didn't update.

Ironwood downloads updates in the background. Close it and open it again, and the new version loads.

The rest timer doesn't beep with the screen locked.

Web apps can't schedule sounds while the phone is locked. Turn on Beep with the phone locked (beta) in Profile, or keep Ironwood on screen. The App Store version will use proper lock-screen alerts.

Are the projections accurate?

They're estimates from your own logged sets, scaled by how consistently you train. They aren't medical or training advice.
