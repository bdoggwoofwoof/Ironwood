Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Ironwood keeps your training data on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect, sell or share your information.

What Ironwood stores, and where

Everything you enter stays in the app's storage on your device:

  • Workouts, sets, notes, routines, personal records and settings.
  • Bodyweight entries, challenges and timers you set up.
  • Progress photos you take or add. Photos are never uploaded.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives it.

Camera and photos

Ironwood asks for the camera only when you open a progress-photo check-in or tap Scan a barcode in the food log. It uses the camera only to take that photo or read that barcode, on your phone; camera images are never sent anywhere. If you pick a photo from your library, only that photo is read. Photos you save to your Photos app or a file go where you choose to send them.

Food lookups (optional, off until you allow them)

When you scan a barcode, Ironwood first checks the foods saved on your phone. If you allow online lookups, it can also ask Open Food Facts, a free public food database, for a product it doesn't know. Only the barcode number, or the words you type into the online search, are sent. Nothing about you, your account, your workouts or your food log goes with it. Like any website, Open Food Facts may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses; see its privacy policy. A product you log is saved on your phone. You can turn lookups off at any time in Log food → Scan.

Backups

When you tap Save backup file, Ironwood creates a file with your data and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes next, such as Files, iCloud Drive or a message, is your choice and is covered by that service's own privacy terms. Restoring reads a backup file you pick.

Feedback

If you use Send feedback, Ironwood prepares a message and opens the Ironwood support page on GitHub, where you can post it as a public issue. Nothing is sent until you post it yourself. The message includes only what you type; if you turn on Include app version, device model and recent error messages, it also includes the app version, your device model and screen size, and the text of recent in-app error messages, all of which would be visible on the public issue. Your workouts, photos and notes are never included. Posting on GitHub is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

The website the app loads from

The web version of Ironwood is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, when your device downloads the app or its updates. See GitHub's privacy statement. Apart from the optional food lookups above, Ironwood loads no other outside services; its fonts and code come from the same site.

Health information

Strength estimates and projections in Ironwood are estimates from your own logged sets (for example, the Epley one-rep-max formula). They are not medical or training advice. Ironwood does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Children

Ironwood is a general fitness app and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Deleting your data

You can delete any workout or photo inside the app. To remove everything, delete Ironwood from your home screen; this erases all of its stored data from the phone. Save a backup first if you want to keep it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on the Ironwood support page.
